Welcome to PaedsQuest

An interactive space to practise clinical reasoning through branching paediatric scenarios — where your decisions shape what happens next.

PaedsQuest is being developed to explore how branching-outcome clinical scenarios can support students in gathering information, interpreting findings, making decisions and learning from the consequences of those decisions.

This is an early trial of the learning experience. Your participation and feedback will help us assess and improve the scenarios before wider use.

Learning objectives

By working through the cases, you will practise how to:

  1. Recognise important clinical cues from a patient's presentation.
  2. Take a focused history and prioritise relevant examination findings.
  3. Interpret clinical information and investigations in context.
  4. Formulate and refine differential diagnoses.
  5. Choose safe management steps, reassess the patient and reflect on the outcome.

Instructions for students

  1. Open Case 1 – The Breathless Child and select an available scenario (1A–1E).
  2. Enter your matriculation number when the scenario requests it. No separate portal login is required.
  3. Make clinical decisions and read the feedback after each choice.
  4. Continue to the case ending, submit your results when prompted and complete the feedback form.
  5. Return to the case selection page to view your completion star.
